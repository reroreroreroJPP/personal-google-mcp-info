Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

These personal tools are operated by one individual for that individual's own Google accounts. They are not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

With the account owner's consent, the tools use Google OAuth scopes for Gmail read, compose, send, and modification; Google Calendar event and calendar-list access; and Google Tasks access. The owner can use the tools to view or change their own messages, events, and tasks.

How data is handled

The tools run on the owner's computer and communicate directly with Google APIs. OAuth tokens are kept in owner-only local configuration files. The tools do not operate a hosted backend or sell Google user data. When the owner invokes these tools through an AI assistant, information the owner chooses to retrieve may be processed by that assistant under its own privacy terms.

Sharing and retention

The tools do not independently share Google data with other people. Changes the owner makes through the tools are applied to their Google account. Local OAuth tokens remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes access. Google handles data under its own policies.

Security and deletion

Access can be revoked in the Google Account security settings. The owner can delete the local token files to remove locally stored authorization credentials. The tools do not maintain a separate hosted copy of Google data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
